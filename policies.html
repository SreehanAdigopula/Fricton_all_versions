Friction Desk Notes

Privacy & Prototype Use

How this browser-based study prototype saves data and contacts other services. Updated October 7, 2026.

In Short

Study progress saves in this browser. The page also contacts the outside services listed below, and people using the same browser profile can see saved data.

What Friction Saves

  • In this browser: Friction saves session and timer state, completion and failure history, distraction and break counts, adaptive notes, pet progress, themes and settings, selected media links, parked thoughts, task information, motivation goals, generated motivation text, and a generated search link.
  • Earlier planning data: If you used the separate System Builder during testing, its goals, plans, and check-ins may also remain in this browser.
  • No account or sync: The current focus app does not have a login, app database, or cross-device sync. It does not send saved study progress to an app backend.

Access, Deletion & Loss

  • Shared browser: Anyone with access to the same unlocked browser profile can read or change saved Friction data. Friction does not encrypt its local storage or protect it with a password. Avoid entering sensitive information.
  • Delete: Settings → Reset Local Data requires the exact word CLEAR, then removes the focus app and earlier System Builder records from this browser. It does not clear unrelated browser data or data held by other services.
  • Data loss: Clearing site data, using a private or temporary tab, changing browsers or devices, or blocked storage can erase or prevent saved progress. There is no backup, export, or recovery feature.

Requests to Other Services

  • Site and fonts: Opening Friction requests pages and files from its Vercel host and font files from Google Fonts. Those providers can receive ordinary request information such as your IP address.
  • Clock: Friction requests the current time from WorldTimeAPI's IP endpoint. That service receives the request and can see your IP address. The app uses your device clock if it fails.
  • Study media: The built-in YouTube player can load when Friction opens, even before you play it. Selecting a custom YouTube or Spotify playlist loads that provider's embed. The provider receives the media identifier and ordinary browser request information; playback and sign-in depend on that provider.
  • Custom links: Only HTTPS YouTube playlist pages and Spotify playlists are accepted. Regular videos, auto-generated YouTube mixes, Spotify tracks, and other websites are not accepted as custom links.
  • Motivation search: If the motivation feature is available and you choose to open its visual ideas link, Friction puts your goal text in a Google Images search URL. Opening it sends that search to Google. Generating motivation text within Friction does not itself run that search.

Prototype Use

  • Friction is a student prototype. Timers, media, and saved progress can fail or change, and it does not promise better grades or productivity.
