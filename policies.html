Friction Desk Notes

Policies

Clear notes about browser storage, media links, and third-party services inside Friction.

Friendly Version

Plain-language notes for how this student prototype handles local progress and media.

Account & Data Policy

  • Saved App Data: Friction saves sessions, distractions, breaks, completions, pet progress, themes, settings, and media choices in this browser.
  • Local Only: Progress does not require an account and does not sync to another device.
  • No App Database: Friction is not sending focus progress to a hosted app database right now.
  • Minimal Data: The app stores only what it needs to run the focus experience on this device.

Storage Policy

  • No Login: Users can open the focus app without creating an account.
  • Browser Storage: Friction uses local browser storage for progress, settings, pet state, and saved study links.
  • Device Bound: Progress stays with the browser that created it unless a future export or sync feature is added.
  • Reset Control: The Settings page can reset local Friction progress for this browser.

Music & Media Policy

  • Focus Environment: Only one built-in or custom study source should be active at a time.
  • Custom Links: You can save YouTube, YouTube Music, or Spotify study media links in this browser.
  • Third-Party Limits: YouTube and Spotify may block embeds, require sign-in, show previews, or change playback behavior.
  • Appropriate Use: Custom media should be safe, study-friendly, and not used to harass or mislead others.

Privacy & Reliability Policy

  • No Selling Data: Friction does not sell focus data or personal progress information.
  • Current Time: The app may use an online time API, and falls back to the device clock if the API fails.
  • Browser Storage: Clearing browser data may remove progress and saved settings.
  • Future Controls: Export and optional sync controls may be added in a future version.
